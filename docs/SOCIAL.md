## LinkedIn Post (Max 300 words)

Most financial cybercrime and mule account detection systems fail for one simple reason: they evaluate bank accounts in flat rows and tabular columns. But organized cyber fraud syndicates don't operate in silos—they operate in multi-layered, multi-district graphs. 🕸️💳

For the **Bihar Police Cyber Hackathon**, our team built **FraudSight**—an autonomous AI/ML and Graph Analytics engine powered by TigerGraph, LangGraph, and Google Gemini, custom-engineered for the **Bihar Police Cyber Crime Division / Economic Offences Unit (EOU)**.

FraudSight addresses the core problem statement end-to-end:
1. 🔍 **Transaction Analysis:** Detects rapid fund layering and velocity smurfing within minutes of cyber fraud execution.
2. 🛡️ **Dynamic Mule Scoring & XAI:** Real-time risk scoring (0.0 to 1.0) with explainable indicator breakdown.
3. 💸 **Multi-Hop Money Trail:** Traces fund flow across Layer-1, Layer-2, and cashout nodes with sub-second TigerGraph GSQL BFS queries.
4. 🔗 **Complaint Correlation:** Correlates transactions across separate 1930 Helpline tickets and NCRP cybercrime complaints to unmask the parent syndicate.
5. 📊 **Law Enforcement Cockpit:** High-performance Cytoscape graph visualizer rendering syndicate clusters, hardware fingerprints, and burner IP subnets.
6. ⚖️ **Evidentiary Dossiers:** Instant generation of Section 65B Indian Evidence Act compliant court-ready certificates, Section 91 CrPC notices, and 1930 CFCFRMS debit freeze orders.
